# Email templates

Starting points only. Rewrite each one in the person's own voice and cut anything that doesn't apply. The person sends from their own mailbox. Keep each email short: one request per email, with the exact URL or record ID so the recipient can act without replying to ask.

Placeholders are in {CURLY_CAPS}.

## Erasure request to a data broker (UK or EU GDPR)

> **Subject:** Erasure request under UK GDPR Articles 17 and 21: {FULL_NAME}
>
> Hello,
>
> I object to your processing of my personal data and ask you to erase it, under Articles 21 and 17 of the UK GDPR. Your online form returned an error, so I am writing instead.
>
> The data concerns me, {FULL_NAME}. You may hold it under {EMAIL_ADDRESSES} and {PHONE_NUMBERS}, and possibly my LinkedIn profile {LINKEDIN_URL}.
>
> Please delete all personal data you hold about me, stop processing it, tell any third parties you have shared it with, and confirm in writing when this is done. I understand you have one month to respond.
>
> Thank you,
> {FULL_NAME}

For US residents, replace the legal basis with the relevant state law, for example "under the California Consumer Privacy Act, I request deletion of the personal information you hold about me".

## Correction request to a page host

For a live page that presents stale information as current, such as a board, trustee or team page. Name the page, quote the stale line and give the replacement.

> **Subject:** Small update to my bio on {SITE}
>
> Hi {NAME},
>
> The bio on {PAGE_URL} still describes me as {STALE_TEXT}. Could you update it? A current version is below, and I'm happy for you to trim it.
>
> {CURRENT_BIO}
>
> Thanks,
> {FIRST_NAME}

## Wrong byline (Muck Rack and similar aggregators)

> **Subject:** Byline correction: I was quoted, not the author
>
> Hello,
>
> Your profile page for me ({PROFILE_URL}) lists me as an author of "{ARTICLE_TITLE}" ({OUTLET}, {DATE}). I did not write that article; it quoted me. Please remove my name from the byline and from my profile: {ARTICLE_URL}.
>
> Thank you,
> {FULL_NAME}

## Company secretary: director details (UK CH01)

For a board or trustee seat at an organisation the person doesn't control.

> **Subject:** Companies House: update to my director details
>
> Hi {NAME},
>
> Companies House records my {DETAIL, e.g. nationality} for {COMPANY} as {CURRENT_VALUE}. It should read {CORRECT_VALUE}. Could you file a CH01 for me, or pass this to whoever handles your Companies House filings? {IF_RELEVANT: I'd also like my service address to be {SERVICE_ADDRESS} rather than my home address.}
>
> Thanks,
> {FIRST_NAME}

>

> {FULL_NAME}

## Request to a platform to identify or release an email address

> **Subject:** Data request: account linked to {EMAIL}
>
> Hello,
>
> The email address {EMAIL} is mine, but it is attached to an X account I cannot identify or access. Under UK GDPR Articles 15 to 17, please tell me which account it is linked to, and either give me access to it or remove my address from it so that I can use the address on my current account, {HANDLE}.
>
> Thank you,
> {FULL_NAME}
